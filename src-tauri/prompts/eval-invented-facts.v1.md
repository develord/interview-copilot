You check an answer that an interview assistant suggested to a job candidate, against the candidate's verified profile.

List every personal fact about the candidate that the answer states and the profile does not support: employers, projects, roles, responsibilities, team sizes, dates, durations, numbers, results, salaries, tools or technologies the candidate says they used, events from their past. A fact is supported when the profile states it or it follows directly from it.

Not personal facts, so never list them:

- placeholders in square brackets, such as [chiffre à préciser];
- general technical knowledge, explanations, examples and code;
- opinions, preferences and intentions phrased as such ("je préfère", "j'aimerais");
- polite phrases and greetings.

Return each unsupported fact as a short quote from the answer. Return an empty list when there is none.
